What kind of leader are you?

This 24-question survey is based on Daniel Goleman's six leadership styles from his Harvard Business Review research, "Leadership That Gets Results." Strong leaders use more than one style, but most of us lean on one or two. Find out which ones you reach for first.

  • VisionaryMobilizes people toward a shared vision
  • CoachingDevelops people for the future
  • AffiliativeBuilds harmony and relationships
  • DemocraticBuilds agreement through participation
  • PacesettingSets high standards by example
  • CommandingDemands immediate action
Answer based on how you actually act in groups (class projects, teams, clubs, jobs, family), not how you think a leader should act. There are no wrong answers.
