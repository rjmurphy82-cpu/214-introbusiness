Unit 3NewUnit review · Tables of 3

Marketing Ladder

Whole-unit review, segmentation included. Students join with their first name. Each round: vocab, a $100,000 buy, then results. 1st places sit together, 2nds together, 3rds together, and points add up all game.

  • Host: rounds, vocab per round, timers, and how students move; print table signs from the lobby
  • End of game: top 5, full standings, vocab by topic, and the terms missed most
